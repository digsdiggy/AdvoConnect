About AdvoConnect

Advocacy starts
with listening.

We work alongside people, helping them understand information, explore their options, express their views and communicate what matters to them.

AdvoConnect is a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation (SCIO) dedicated to advancing health and relieving those in need by reason of ill-health through the provision of independent mental health advocacy.

We support people to have their voices heard when decisions are being made about their health, wellbeing, care and support.

Our Mission

To empower people experiencing ill-health to have their voices heard, understand their rights and participate meaningfully in decisions that affect their lives.

Through independent advocacy, we aim to reduce barriers that can prevent people from expressing their views and ensure that the people we support remain at the centre of decisions about their lives.

We work alongside people, not on their behalf without their involvement.

Looking ahead

A society where
everyone has a voice.

Everyone should be listened to and everyone should be able to participate in decisions about their own life.

We envision a future where people experiencing mental ill-health are treated with dignity and respect, their views are taken seriously, and they have access to independent support when they need it.

AdvoConnect wants to contribute to a fairer, more inclusive society where people are empowered to speak for themselves and exercise meaningful choice and control.

What guides us

Our Objectives

Our objectives are centred on improving people's ability to participate, make informed choices and have their voices heard.

Provide independent advocacy to people experiencing mental ill-health and other forms of ill-health.

Support people to express their views, wishes and concerns.

Help people understand information relating to decisions about their health, wellbeing and support.

Promote people's participation in decisions that affect their lives.

Promote equality, dignity, respect and inclusion.

Help reduce barriers that can prevent people from being heard.

Work collaboratively with individuals, communities and relevant organisations.

Raise awareness of the importance and value of independent advocacy.

Learn from the experiences of people who use our services to improve what we do.

Substance Use
Advocacy

People who experience challenges relating to alcohol or other substances deserve to be listened to and treated with dignity and respect.

We work alongside individuals without judgement, recognising that everyone's circumstances and goals are different.

Your past does not define your right to be heard.
↗

Prison
Advocacy

Our advocacy service provides independent support that helps people express their views and ensure those views are properly communicated.

We can support people to understand information, prepare for conversations and meetings, raise concerns and participate in relevant decisions.

Everyone deserves to have their voice heard, wherever they are.
